MEET YASHU

Yashu is an annoying yet loving woman. At times she gets on my nerves, at times she calms them. She is a figure of ultimate ambition to motivate generations of women to stand strong and bold. Although she's a timid sparrer, she strives to exemplify in power lifting. While she can be wonky somedays, she's dances Bharatnatyam with blissful elegance. Anyone who watches shall be mesmerized or do I mean hypnotized? Gues we'll never know. You see Yashu online and you think "Oh god she's scary," but she's the sweetest woman you'll ever come across - comparable to most mothers, fearful but beloved. Yashu!..nah... her real name is Yasaswini; ikr beautiful... but don't call her that or she'll unleash her powerpuff girl powers. To have her in your presence is mesmerizing, untill she gets hungry, then she's hangry. And trust me. You don't want to deal with hangry Yasaswini. I call her Goldie, because although cute, she reminds me of a Goldfish. Well... do you agree? Let's ask Ishanuuu....

But anyhow, this woman's gonna do wonders one day. Idgaf if y'all don't believe it. I believe it. But one thing's for sure, she's gonna forget all about lil ol' me in a couple months I bet.

Yasaswini Gurazada everyone! Whoop Whoop!
